## How this document is organised

This is a build-ready copy deck. Each section below maps to a block on the website, in top-to-bottom page order. For every block you get:

- **Purpose** — why the section exists
- **Copy** — headlines, subheads, body, button labels (ready to paste)
- **Image / visual direction** — what to show and how it should feel

A note on benchmarking: the structure follows what the best school-operating-system and edu-fintech sites do well (PowerSchool, Blackbaud, Toddle, Classe365, Gradelink, plus African leaders like Edves, Flutterwave-for-Business and SchoolTry) — a sharp value promise up top, role-based storytelling, a fintech/ROI angle, hard proof, transparent pricing, and low-friction conversion. CASPAA's differentiators (offline-first, integrated payments & reconciliation, digital consent, and business intelligence for owners) are pulled forward everywhere, because that is where you win.

# Appendix A — Brand & tone guidelines for the writer/designer

**Brand colours (from the deck):**
- Primary: Deep forest green (`#0E4D3C`-ish) and darker green backgrounds
- Accent: Bright yellow/gold (for emphasis, CTAs, premium tier)
- Neutrals: White, off-white, soft grey for light sections
- Data-viz: Pink/magenta + blue (as in the enrolment donut)

**Voice & tone:**
- **Confident, not boastful.** State outcomes plainly.
- **Owner-first.** Lead with revenue, control and visibility — that's who signs.
- **Concrete over vague.** "Pay in 30 seconds," "reconcile in real time," "loans in 24 hours."
- **African context is a strength, not a caveat.** Offline-first and fintech are hero features.
- **Short sentences. Active voice. Real numbers.**

**Recurring taglines to reuse:**
- *The all-in-one digital infrastructure for African schools.*
- *One platform for every part of your school.*
- *Most systems tell you what happened. CASPAA helps you decide what happens next.*
- *Smart schools run on systems. Great schools run on CASPAA.*

**Design principles (benchmarked to top SaaS sites):**
- Alternate light and dark sections for rhythm.
- One primary CTA repeated consistently ("Book a Demo").
- Real product screenshots > abstract illustrations wherever possible.
- Generous whitespace; premium, fintech-grade feel.
- Every section earns the scroll — lead each with a benefit, not a feature.
