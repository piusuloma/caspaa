Strategic Positioning Statement

CASPAA is not simply a school management system — it is a School Operating System with a built-in financial infrastructure layer that no African EdTech platform currently offers at the same depth. Our fee lending, reconciliation engine, SaaS operator dashboard, and digital consent workflows are genuine differentiators. The priority now is to close the learning content gap (video lessons, class notes, simulations) and complete the operational modules (transport, hostel, health) so that CASPAA is a complete platform — not just a strong one.

Financial Infrastructure
Fee lending, reconciliation, multi-role payroll, real-time P&L — deepest in market.
Platform Architecture
True SaaS operator layer with school health scoring, MRR/ARR tracking, and remittances.
Learning Content
Gap to close: video lessons, simulations, class notes, virtual classroom integration.
02 — Competitive Landscape
Where We Lead vs. Where We Lag

A side-by-side view of CASPAA's strengths and the gaps that need to be addressed in the next product phase.

CASPAA Advantages — Features EDVES Does Not Have
  • Fee Lending & Financing Module — parents apply for fee loans; full repayment schedule tracking
  • Platform Super Admin Layer — MRR, ARR, school health scores, remittance management
  • Digital Consent Forms — paperless activity & trip approvals with parent tracking
  • Student Gamification — stars, points, achievement badges driving engagement
  • Substitute Teacher Coverage Workflow — structured coverage requests and acceptance
  • Role Impersonation — admins can view the system exactly as any user role sees it
Critical Gaps — Features EDVES Has That We Are Missing
  • Video Lessons & E-Videos — student and teacher video content library
  • Virtual Classroom — live online classes (currently a stub)
  • Class Notes for Students — teacher-posted notes accessible in student portal
  • Maths & Science Simulations (PhET) — interactive science labs
  • School Bus & Transport Management — route tracking, parent notifications
  • Hostel Management — boarding student records and room assignments
  • House Point System — dedicated pastoral care module
  • Parent Feedback Tool — structured parent-to-school feedback forms
  • Secure Pickup (Parent Portal) — approved pickup person management
  • Event Calendar — school-wide events visible across all roles
  • Teacher Payslip View — teachers viewing their own salary statements
  • Communication Diary — structured daily teacher ↔ parent notes
  • Formative Assessment — distinct in-class ongoing assessment (not just CBT)
  • Bulk SMS & Email — currently marked TBD in codebase

05 — Conclusion
Recommendation

CASPAA enters the market with a stronger financial and platform infrastructure than EDVES. The single most important action is to close the learning content gap — because that is what students and parents interact with daily, and it is the primary reason schools choose EDVES today.

Immediate Focus (Next 60 Days)
  • Wire up online payment gateway (Paystack/Flutterwave)
  • Build Event Calendar visible across all roles
  • Implement Class Notes in the student learning hub
  • Add Video Lesson embedding per subject
  • Integrate PhET Simulations for Maths & Science
  • Activate Bulk SMS via Africa's Talking or Termii
Medium-Term Focus (60–120 Days)
  • Dedicate House Point System as its own module
  • Build Transport Management + Secure Pickup
  • Add Parent Feedback Tool
  • Complete Inventory & Health/Sickbay stubs
  • Add Communication Diary for teacher-parent notes
  • Begin AI roadmap: question generation from lesson notes
